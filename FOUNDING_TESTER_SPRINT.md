# Founding Tester Sprint

Status: **FROZEN UNTIL BOTH V0.4 EVIDENCE GATES PASS**

Recruit five real AI-video creators after Gate 9 and Gate 10 pass. Each tester
uses a fresh clone and one already-generated clip of their own to complete:

```text
observe → diff → compile-next
```

The tester is successful when, within 15 minutes, they install or run the
Skill, bind their accepted video and final frame, record all six observations,
and obtain a continuity-safe next-shot contract and Prompt. They do not need to
generate, publish, or submit the resulting video.

Record only anonymous tester ID, platform/Python version, elapsed time,
completion state, blocking step, and optional feedback. Do not collect account
credentials, provider links, private media, or personal identifiers.

At least four of five testers must succeed before the coordinated public
launch. Onboarding failures may trigger documentation or CLI fixes and another
fresh-clone verification. They do not authorize regenerating the frozen A/B
evidence.
